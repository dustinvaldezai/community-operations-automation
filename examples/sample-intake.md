# Synthetic Intake Examples

The messages below are fictional and created only to demonstrate routing logic. They do not represent real community members or real correspondence.

## Message A — routine access question

> Hi, I’m interested in visiting the garden this weekend. What hours are you usually open, and is there anything I need to know before coming by?

## Message B — workshop interest

> Hello, I saw that you sometimes host gardening workshops. I’d like to know how to hear about the next beginner session.

## Message C — volunteer inquiry

> I’m looking for a regular volunteer opportunity and could help a couple of Saturdays each month. Who should I coordinate with?

## Message D — exception request

> I know the usual process is different, but could I get special access outside the normal schedule next week?

## Message E — complaint / interpersonal issue

> I had a difficult interaction with another participant and would like to speak with someone about what happened.

## Demonstration goal

A good workflow should not treat these five messages as equivalent. The first three may be candidates for structured routine handling. The last two should be routed to human review because they require judgment and context.
