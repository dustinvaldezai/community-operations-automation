## Illustrative draft for Message B

> Thanks for reaching out. We do host beginner-friendly gardening workshops from time to time. I can share the current signup or notification process once the latest schedule is confirmed. If you’d like, your interest can also be logged for follow-up.

## Why Message D stops

The sender is asking for an exception to a normal process. Even if the wording looks simple, the response would create a decision or commitment. The workflow should therefore escalate rather than generate an approval.

## Why Message E stops

Complaints and interpersonal issues can involve context, safety, privacy, and fairness. The system may help acknowledge receipt, but it should not decide the substance of the response.
